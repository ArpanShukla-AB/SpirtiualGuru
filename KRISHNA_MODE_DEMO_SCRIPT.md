## 🎤 Handling Q&A

### Q1: "How does emotion detection work?"

**Answer:**
> "We use keyword matching primarily—very hackathon-friendly. In production, we'd upgrade to LLM-based detection. For example, 'fail', 'defeated', 'hopeless' all map to the **Failure** emotion. Then we match that emotion to a relevant Gita concept like **Equanimity**."

---

### Q2: "What makes this different from ChatGPT?"

**Answer:**
> "Three things:
> 
> 1. **Framework**: We structure responses into teaching + understanding + action steps
> 2. **Philosophy**: Every response is grounded in Gita principles, not generic advice
> 3. **Purpose**: We're a decision-support system, not a general chatbot"

---

### Q3: "Can it scale?"

**Answer:**
> "Yes. The system is:
> - Database-driven (700 shlokas indexed by emotion)
> - Component-based (easy to add features)
> - API-ready (can call any LLM backend)
> - Currently deployed on Vite, ready for production"

---

### Q4: "What's your unique angle?"

**Answer:**
> "Most AI solutions are generic. We're using ancient wisdom as the framework. That's rare. A student gets not just AI-generated advice, but advice grounded in 2000-year-old philosophy that's been tested on generations. That's powerful."
